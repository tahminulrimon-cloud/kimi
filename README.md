# 17 Fd Regt — Unit Accounts Handover & Organization

Working repo for reviewing and organizing unit account documents received
during handover as 2IC / Account Officer, 17 Fd Regt, Cumilla Cantonment.

## How this is organized

- `documents/` — copies of received documents, sorted into subfolders by
  account head (see below). Drop scanned/photographed copies into the
  matching folder as you receive them.
- `ACCOUNTS_TRACKER.md` — a checklist of the standard account heads a unit
  account officer normally holds, with status columns to mark what's been
  received, reviewed, and any action needed. Update this as documents come in.
- `HANDOVER_PLAN.md` — a phased plan for taking over and organizing the
  account holding.

## Document folders

| Folder | Covers |
|---|---|
| `public-fund-imprest` | Imprest cash account, bank pass book/statements, cash book, PAO correspondence |
| `non-public-funds` | Unit/Regimental Fund, Officers' Mess Fund, JCOs' Mess Fund, Sainik/OR Institute Fund, Welfare Fund, Sports/Library funds |
| `ration-account` | Dry/wet ration ledgers, condiment account, cook-house registers |
| `mes-barracks` | MES accounts, barracks/married quarters occupation, rent recovery |
| `clothing-stores` | Clothing & necessaries account, G-1098 stores where it touches accounts |
| `pay-irla-part2` | IRLA-related unit records, Part II Orders affecting pay/allowances |
| `csd-canteen` | CSD canteen fund and stock accounts |
| `audit-loss-statements` | Internal/external audit objections, loss statements, Court of Inquiry financial findings, their compliance status |
| `handover-takeover` | The actual handing/taking over certificate(s) and annexures from the outgoing Acting 2IC |
| `misc` | Anything not yet classified — I'll re-sort once reviewed |

Upload each document (photo/scan/PDF) and tell me which folder it belongs
to, or just upload it — I'll read it, tell you which head it falls under,
log it in the tracker, flag anything that looks incomplete/discrepant, and
move it to the right folder.
