# Handover & Organization Plan

## Phase 1 — Immediate (first 1–2 weeks)
- Physically verify cash-in-hand for imprest and every fund against the
  outgoing officer's declared balances, on the day of takeover — not later.
- Obtain and sign the formal Handing/Taking Over Certificate only after
  every balance is verified; do not sign against outstanding discrepancies —
  record them as exceptions on the certificate instead.
- Collect bank statements/pass books for every account and reconcile
  against the respective cash books.
- Get the complete, dated list of outstanding audit objections and loss
  statements — these carry timelines and follow-up is time-critical.
- Confirm there is no unresolved Court of Inquiry / Board of Officers
  financial matter left pending action.

## Phase 2 — Short-term (first month)
- Cross-check every fund's last internal audit board proceedings — if
  overdue, convene one.
- Verify subscription registers (mess funds, welfare fund, PF, insurance)
  are current and reconcile against nominal roll strength.
- Confirm Part II Orders affecting pay/allowances have been correctly
  reflected and there's no backlog.
- Review MES barracks/quarters occupation list against actual
  strength/married accommodation allotment.

## Phase 3 — Medium-term (ongoing systematization)
- Standardize a monthly closing routine for every fund/account (cash book
  closing, bank reconciliation, fund balance report to CO).
- Set a recurring internal check (e.g., monthly surprise cash count,
  quarterly audit board) rather than relying only on the annual audit.
- Keep a running register of audit objections with target closure dates
  and owner (self-tracked, reviewed monthly).
- Maintain this repo (or equivalent) as the running digital index of
  physical registers — not a replacement for them, but a cross-reference
  so nothing is "lost" between postings.

## Phase 4 — Steady state
- Monthly: fund balance report, cash count, bank reconciliation.
- Quarterly: audit board for non-public funds, review of outstanding
  objections.
- Annually: formal audit, PF/insurance reconciliation, review this plan
  and update the tracker's account-head list against actual local
  Regimental Standing Orders / PAFA instructions (this list is a general
  starting framework — adjust it to whatever your unit's/PAO's actual
  requirements are).

## Note on scope
This framework reflects standard/generic unit-level financial
administration structure. Cumilla Cantt / 17 Fd Regt may have local
Regimental Standing Orders, Formation instructions, or PAO-specific
formats that differ — treat the tracker's account-head list as a
checklist to validate against those, not a substitute for them.
